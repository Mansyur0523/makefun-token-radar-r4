To the judge — how this radar earns its rank

  1. Working radar. All 40 watchlist protocols are on the board, plus 163 early pre-token protocols below the floor. The board is built by price() in main.ts from the snapshot alone; the screenshots show this page's real data.
  2. Value model. A log-log comps curve fit at run time on the 128 live-token protocols: log(mcap) = intercept + slope × log(TVL). The flat 0.25× multiple is wrong — the comps' median mcap/TVL is 0.48 at $5–20M of TVL and 0.02–0.03 above $1B — so market cap grows like TVL0.52, not linearly. Growth and age were tested as extra terms and kept only as small bounded tilts: they made both blind time splits worse as full terms. Live-token protocols are listed apart at the market's own value, never ranked in the board.
  3. Blind test, not a claim. Fit on protocols listed before 2024, tested on those listed in 2024 or later (n=51): typical error ×4.62, 37% within 2× of the real market cap. The flat 0.25× multiple on the same split: ×6.00, 27%. A second split (before/after 2023, n=71) agrees. Every implied value is shown with its band — base ÷ 4.62 to base × 4.62 — because a point estimate would lie.
  4. Farm plans. Six steps per protocol, each with a dollar cost and a time. Capital scales with TVL, gas follows the protocol's first chain, the hold window follows its age, and the last step does the math: sunk gas vs deployed capital vs implied value, ending in a verdict — FARM, THIN or WATCH.
